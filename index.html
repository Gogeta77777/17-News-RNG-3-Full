Community Guidelines

To play, please read and agree to these community guidelines. 17 News moderation is active 24/7.

  • Do not exploit glitches, abuse bugs, or use unintended game mechanics for an unfair advantage. Report issues instead.
  • Do not steal or attempt to steal accounts, coins, items, or other players’ property.
  • Use one personal account. Do not share, sell, buy, or lend accounts or passwords, or use someone else’s account.
  • Do not hack, cheat, use unauthorized scripts, or interfere with the game or its services.
  • Do not bully, harass, threaten, discriminate against, or impersonate other players.
  • Keep chat respectful: no hate speech, explicit content, scams, spam, or sharing another person’s private information.
  • Do not evade moderation or encourage others to break these rules. Use common sense and treat the community fairly.

Breaking these rules may result in a warning, ban, or worse. Moderation decisions are made to protect the community.
